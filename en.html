MotionSky Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026  ·  Effective: October 29, 2026

MotionSky (体感天空, Android package com.openmc.balancesky, the "Game") is developed and operated by zhaolidong, an individual developer ("we"). This policy explains how the Game handles information. If the Chinese version and this English version differ, the Chinese version prevails.

1. Summary

MotionSky is an offline, single-player motion-control game. It has no account system, does not request network access, contains no ads, in-app purchases or third-party SDKs, and does not collect, upload or share any personal information.

2. Information used on your device

InformationPurposeHandling
Sensor list (sensor types your device supports)Check whether gravity, accelerometer and gyroscope sensors exist and choose the motion inputRead once on your device after you agree to this policy; never stored or uploaded
Accelerometer (gravity) dataTilt controls for the ball, car, crosshair and football; calibrationProcessed in real time on your device only; never stored or uploaded
Gyroscope data (if available)"Rotate to aim" in the shooting modeProcessed in real time on your device only; never stored or uploaded
Game settings and progress (sensitivity, sound, vibration, language, level stars, best scores)Remember your preferences and progressStored locally on your device only
System languageChoose Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese or English on first launchRead locally only

When: the sensor list, accelerometer and gyroscope are only read after you tap "Agree and continue" in the privacy prompt shown on first launch. Reading stops while the Game is paused or in the background. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy → Withdraw privacy consent, after which the Game stops reading sensors immediately.

We do not collect your name, phone number, ID documents, device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, OAID, IDFA), IP address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.

3. Permissions

Permission / featurePurposeCan be turned off
Accelerometer, gyroscope (including the sensor list)Core motion controls; used only after you agree in the first-launch privacy promptYes, withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy
Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE)Haptic feedback for collisions, shots and hitsYes, in the in-game Settings

The Game does not request network, storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone or SMS permissions.

4. Storage, sharing and third parties

All game data stays in the Game's private storage on your device. We do not share, transfer or disclose personal information to anyone, and no third-party SDKs are integrated. If your Android system backup is enabled, the system may back up app data according to your system settings and the backup provider's privacy policy.

5. Your choices

You can change settings in the in-game Settings page, and delete all local data by clearing the app's data in system settings or uninstalling the Game. There is no account to delete.

6. Children

The Game does not collect personal information from children. Minors, especially those under 14, should use the Game with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian. Please play in a safe place and avoid playing while walking or travelling.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will publish the new version on this page. For material changes (for example, if we start collecting personal information or integrate third-party SDKs), we will notify you prominently in the Game or on the store page and ask for your consent where required.
